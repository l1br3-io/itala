# Product

Who Itala is being built for, and the jobs they want to accomplish.

---

## Personas

- **Individual User** — Creates and manages personal vaults of notes. May use the system offline, sync across devices, or publish selected content.
- **Collaborator (Invited Only)** — A person granted access to a shared vault or subset of notes. Permissions are scoped and revocable.
- **Plugin Developer** — Creates extensions (editors, themes, sync providers, AI integrations) and publishes them to the community hub.
- **Organization Admin** — Manages team vaults, policies, and plugin allowlists (future scope).

---

## User Stories

- **As a researcher,** I want to keep all my notes, PDFs, and annotations in one place, linked together, so I can trace ideas across papers and my own writing.
- **As a developer,** I want to write documentation in Markdown, preview it live, and export to a static site, all while keeping everything versioned in Git.
- **As a writer,** I want a distraction-free writing environment that helps me connect related notes and visualize the structure of my work.
- **As a student,** I want to sync my notes between my laptop and phone so I can review on the go, and I want to search everything instantly.
- **As a privacy-conscious user,** I want all my data to stay on my own devices, with no cloud dependency, but still have access to modern features like linking and search.
- **As a plugin developer,** I want to create a custom visualization for my notes and share it with the community via the hub.
- **As a team lead,** I want to maintain a shared knowledge base for my team, with clear version history and optional sync to our own server.
